What the motion alleges

The motion cites provisions of the temporary custody order involving major decisions, the alternating-week schedule, travel information, fostering the child’s relationship with the other parent, and overnight right of first refusal. It alleges noncompliance in several areas, including:

  • Overnight care and the right-of-first-refusal provision.
  • Adherence to the alternating-week physical custody schedule.
  • Spring break and vacation scheduling.
  • Sharing information about the child, medical appointments, mental health, school absences, and travel.

These points summarize the filing’s assertions. They are not independent conclusions about whether a violation occurred.

Relief requested

The motion asks the court to find civil and/or criminal contempt, award attorney’s fees associated with the filing, and grant other appropriate relief. The combined PDF also includes the July 2024 custody order as an exhibit.
