What the order establishes

The Wake County District Court order records the parties’ agreement concerning temporary child custody. It states that both parents share joint legal custody and should discuss and attempt to agree on major decisions involving health, welfare, education, and religion.

  • An alternating-week physical custody schedule beginning in August 2024, with Sunday evening exchanges unless the parties mutually agree otherwise.
  • Holiday and special-event provisions covering Thanksgiving, winter break, Mother’s Day, Father’s Day, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, and Passover.
  • Equal access to school, medical, mental-health, and other professional records and providers.
  • Notice requirements for emergencies, contact changes, and travel with the child.
  • A requirement that both parents avoid disparaging remarks and attempt to foster respect, love, and admiration for the other parent.
  • A right-of-first-refusal provision when either parent cannot care for the child overnight.
